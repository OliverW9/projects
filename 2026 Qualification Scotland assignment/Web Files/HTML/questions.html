Frequently asked questions


What is an escape room?

An escape room is an interactive, immersive experience where you and your group solve puzzles, uncover clues, and work together to complete a mission within a set time limit. Each room has a unique storyline and theme, making every adventure a new challenge!


How do I book a room?

Booking is simple! Visit our Contact Us Page and select your preferred room and challenge.


How many people can play at once?

Each room has a recommended team size, typically ranging from 1 to 5 players. For larger groups or special events, please contact us, and we’ll help you arrange a setup that works best for everyone.


Are the rooms scary?

We offer a variety of themes, some of which include thrilling or suspenseful elements.


What if we don't escape in time?

Don’t worry! Our game masters will review any unsolved puzzles and explain how you could have completed the room. It’s all about having fun and testing your skills, so you’ll leave with a smile no matter the outcome.


Is there an age limit?

Most of our rooms are suitable for ages 12 and up. However, participants under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. For younger children, we recommend contacting us to see if we can make any special accommodations.


Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, we understand that plans can change. To cancel or reschedule, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance to avoid any fees. Cancellations made on short notice may be subject to a small fee.


Do I need to bring anything?

Just bring your sense of adventure! We recommend arriving about 15 minutes early for a briefing. All the tools and clues you’ll need are provided in the room.


Can we book an escape room for special events?

Absolutely! Escape rooms are perfect for birthdays, team-building events, and celebrations. We offer special packages for groups and can customise the experience to make your event memorable. Just reach out to us to plan your next adventure.
