Jungle

The Jungle Room plunges you into a dense, overgrown wilderness filled with ancient relics, mysterious carvings, and hidden clues. As you navigate through lush foliage and twisting vines, you will encounter puzzles that guard the secrets of a lost civilisation. Beware of hidden traps and challenges that test your wit and teamwork at every step. Solve the mysteries and escape the jungle before it swallows you whole!


Carnival

The Carnival Room immerses you in a twisted, whimsical fairground where eerie laughter echoes through the air and secrets lurk behind every stall. Step into a world of distorted mirrors, enigmatic fortune tellers, and faded circus posters that hint at forgotten mysteries. Each game and attraction conceals a clue, but beware—nothing in this carnival is as it seems. Unravel the secrets before the lights flicker out, and the carnival vanishes for good!
